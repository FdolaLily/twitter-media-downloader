# Product

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Twitter/X users saving media through a Tampermonkey userscript. The GIF editor helps
them choose a short segment visually before local conversion.

## Design principles

- Show exactly what is kept and removed using both color and pattern.
- Preview the frame at the user's chosen time and preserve selection length when moving.
- Enforce the 15-second limit during editing with immediate, non-blocking feedback.
- Support pointer, keyboard, and precise time inputs; keep cancellation accessible.
- Use concise Chinese/English labels and the existing system-font dialog design.
